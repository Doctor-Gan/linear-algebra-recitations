# Week 1 — Linear Systems: Solutions, Freedom, and Geometry

**Linear Algebra I · Bowen Gan · Fall 2026**

The first recitation moves from a broad view of mathematics to the geometry of solution sets. The central idea is that a homogeneous solution describes a direction in which a nonhomogeneous solution can move without changing its output.

## Reading route

1. Mathematics and the place of linear algebra.
2. A system with a unique solution and the reversibility of elimination.
3. Homogeneous solutions, particular solutions, and affine planes.
4. Exercise 1.14(2): a parameter-dependent system, solved by elimination and by symmetry.
5. Optional: Exercise 1.15(2), four points on a circle.
6. No solution, a unique solution, or infinitely many solutions.
7. Why full rank, trivial kernel, and unique solvability are equivalent for a square matrix.
